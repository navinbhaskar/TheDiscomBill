How does it work?

  1. You enter each AC's tonnage, star rating and daily hours, plus any other monthly units.
  2. Each AC's running draw = cooling capacity ÷ its ISEER — an inverter AC at part load draws far less than its nameplate watts.
  3. Daily consumption = that draw × your hours, plus your other units ÷ 30.
  4. System size = daily kWh ÷ (your zone's peak sun hours × 0.80 for inverter, heat and dust losses).
  5. Rule of thumb: a 1.5-ton 5-star AC running 8 hours a day needs about 1.2 kW of panels.

A planning guide, not a quote — a site survey by an empanelled vendor on the National Portal ↗ is the final word.
