Smart Meter Recharge Calculator

How long will a ₹500 recharge actually last on your prepaid smart meter? Pick your DISCOM and monthly usage — we run it through the real tariff (slab rates, fixed charges, FPPA, duty) and show your daily burn rate, how many days each recharge buys, and the ideal monthly recharge amount.

How this calculator works

Most "units × rate" estimates get prepaid maths wrong, because a prepaid balance doesn't only pay for energy. Every day your meter also deducts a share of the fixed charge, FPPA (fuel surcharge) and electricity duty. This tool computes your full monthly bill with the same engine as our bill calculator — slab-wise rates and all — then divides by 30 to get the true daily burn rate your balance drains at.
