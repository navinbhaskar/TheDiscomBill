ℹ️ Chandigarh's domestic tariff is unusually flat: the top slab (₹5.40) is under twice the first (₹2.95), where most states more than treble across the same range, so a heavy user here is treated far more gently than in Goa or Uttar Pradesh. Because JERC fixed the whole FY 2025-26 to FY 2029-30 control period in one order, rates step up each year without any fresh order — the same slab reaches ₹5.65 by FY 2029-30. JERC's schedule is the tariff alone. Domestic bills here include Chandigarh's 9 paise/unit electricity duty; other State levies are charged on the bill in addition. The fixed charge is per kW (or kVA) 'or part thereof', so a fractional sanctioned load is rounded up to the next whole unit on a real bill — the calculator does not round.

The energy rates are close to domestic — ₹4.60 against ₹2.95 on the first slab — but the fixed charge is four times higher per unit of load, which is where the real difference sits for a small shop. Frequently asked questions

How is the electricity bill calculated in Chandigarh?
Chandigarh bills combine slab-wise energy charges, a fixed/demand charge per kW (or kVA), a fuel & power purchase adjustment (FPPA) and electricity duty. Use our free calculator to get an itemised provisional bill for your DISCOM.
Which electricity distribution company serves my area in Chandigarh?
Chandigarh is served by 1 DISCOM: CPDL (Chandigarh) (Chandigarh Union Territory). Open your DISCOM above for its full tariff and an indicative bill.
What is the cheapest domestic electricity rate in Chandigarh?
The lowest domestic energy charge across Chandigarh DISCOMs starts at about ₹2.9 per unit (lowest slab), before fixed charges, FPPA and duty. Exact rates depend on your DISCOM and consumption slab.
What is the current electricity tariff year for Chandigarh?
The rates shown are for 2026-27 (latest available).
