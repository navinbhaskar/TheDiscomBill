Frequently asked questions

How is the electricity bill calculated in Maharashtra?
Maharashtra bills combine slab-wise energy charges, a fixed/demand charge per kW (or kVA), a fuel & power purchase adjustment (FPPA) and electricity duty. Use our free calculator to get an itemised provisional bill for your DISCOM.
Which electricity distribution company serves my area in Maharashtra?
Maharashtra is served by 4 DISCOMs: MSEDCL (Maharashtra — except Mumbai city area served by Adani/BEST/Tata Power); Adani Electricity Mumbai (Mumbai suburbs — Bandra, Andheri, Kurla); BEST Mumbai (Mumbai Island City — South Mumbai – Colaba to Mahim/Sion); Tata Power Mumbai (Parts of Mumbai — Dharavi, Wadala, parts of Kurla). Open your DISCOM above for its full tariff and an indicative bill.
What is the cheapest domestic electricity rate in Maharashtra?
The lowest domestic energy charge across Maharashtra DISCOMs starts at about ₹1.9 per unit (lowest slab), before fixed charges, FPPA and duty. Exact rates depend on your DISCOM and consumption slab.
What is the current electricity tariff year for Maharashtra?
The rates shown are for 2026-27 (latest available).
