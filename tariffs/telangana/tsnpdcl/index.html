TGNPDCL tariff schedule (2026-27)

TGNPDCL applies the same state-wide 2026-27 tariff schedule as the other Telangana DISCOMs (set by the state regulator). What differs by company is the service area, billing portal and contact details below.

🏠

LT-I (Domestic)

Fixed charge
up to 800 units/month₹10/kW/mo
above 800 units/month₹50/kW/mo
Energy charges
0 – 200 units₹5.10/unit
200 – 300 units₹7.70/unit
300 – 400 units₹9.00/unit
400 – 800 units₹9.50/unit
Above 800 units₹10.00/unit
Additional charges
  • Electricity Duty (ED)6%

ℹ️ Telangana re-rates the whole bill when consumption crosses 100 or 200 units a month, so a 101-unit bill costs noticeably more than a 100-unit one. There is no separate customer charge for domestic connections. The fixed charge is ₹10 per kW per month, rising to ₹50 per kW above 800 units.

Energy charges
0 – 100 units₹8.50/unit
100 – 300 units₹9.90/unit
300 – 500 units₹10.40/unit
Above 500 units₹11.00/unit
Additional charges
  • Customer charge₹50
  • Electricity Duty (ED)6%

ℹ️ Crossing 50 units a month re-rates the entire bill from ₹7.00 to the ₹8.50 ladder. Single-phase connections pay a ₹50/month customer charge; three-phase pay ₹100. Advertising hoardings (LT-II(C)) are billed separately at ₹13.00/unit.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my TGNPDCL electricity bill?
Open the TGNPDCL bill calculator, enter your units consumed and sanctioned load, and it applies the 2026-27 TGNPDCL slab rates, fixed charge and other charges to produce an itemised provisional bill.
Which areas and cities does TGNPDCL serve?
TGNPDCL (Telangana State Northern Power Distribution Company Ltd.) supplies electricity to Northern Telangana — Warangal, Karimnagar, Khammam, Adilabad, Nizamabad, Jagtial, Peddapalli, Mancherial, Nirmal, Bhadradri Kothagudem, Mahabubabad, Jayashankar Bhupalpally.
What is the cheapest domestic electricity rate on TGNPDCL?
TGNPDCL's domestic energy charge starts at ₹1.95 per unit and rises to ₹10 per unit in the highest slab (LT-I (Domestic)), plus a monthly fixed charge (latest available estimate). The full slab table is above.
How do I pay my TGNPDCL electricity bill online?
Pay on the official TGNPDCL portal at tgnpdcl.in. Use this page first to check what your bill should be, then pay on the authentic source.
