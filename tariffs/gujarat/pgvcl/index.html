How do I calculate my PGVCL electricity bill?
Open the PGVCL bill calculator, enter your units consumed and sanctioned load, and it applies the 2026-27 PGVCL slab rates, fixed charge and other charges to produce an itemised provisional bill.
Which areas and cities does PGVCL serve?
PGVCL (Paschim Gujarat Vij Company Ltd.) supplies electricity to Saurashtra & Kutch — Rajkot, Jamnagar, Junagadh, Porbandar, Bhavnagar, Amreli, Kutch, Surendranagar, Morbi.
What is the cheapest domestic electricity rate on PGVCL?
PGVCL's domestic energy charge starts at ₹2.65 per unit and rises to ₹5.2 per unit in the highest slab (RGP (Residential General Purpose)), plus a monthly fixed charge (latest available estimate). The full slab table is above.
How do I pay my PGVCL electricity bill online?
Pay on the official PGVCL portal at www.pgvcl.com. Use this page first to check what your bill should be, then pay on the authentic source.
