Who each rate applies to

Domestic (rural and urban)
The ordinary household category: four telescopic slabs from ₹4.86 to ₹8.52 after subsidy, on ₹70 per kW per month.
Domestic (rural) — 50 units or fewer
A concessional rural rate of ₹3.94 after subsidy, on ₹70/kW/month. It applies only while consumption stays at or below 50 units in the billing period; above that the ordinary domestic slabs apply.
Kutir Jyoti
The lifeline category: ₹2.31/unit after subsidy on a flat ₹30 per connection per month.
Domestic — three phase (compulsory above 4 kW)
A single ₹8.37 after subsidy on all units, on ₹125/kW/month. Any domestic connection above 4 kW must take three-phase supply and this rate.

ℹ️ Tripura subsidises the bottom of the ladder and nothing above it: 67 paise comes off the first slab, 52 paise off the second, and the 151-300 and 301+ slabs get nothing. Crossing 150 units therefore costs more than the headline rate suggests, because you lose the subsidy as well as moving up a slab. Any domestic connection above 4 kW must take three-phase supply, which is a single flat rate rather than slabs. The rates here are after the Government of Tripura subsidy, which is what your bill charges. The approved tariff behind them is higher — ₹5.53 on the first domestic slab against ₹4.86 billed — and the subsidy tapers to nothing above 150 units, so the top two slabs are already unsubsidised.

Wrong bill in Tripura? Where to complain

Complain in this order and keep the complaint number at every step. Skipping a step is the commonest reason a genuine billing complaint is returned unheard.

StepWhere it goesWhat you need
1. DISCOM complaint deskTSECL, or the 24×7 helpline 1912Consumer number, bill number, the one disputed line, and a meter photo
2. Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF)Your DISCOM's forum, constituted under TERC regulationsThe first complaint number, and what the DISCOM did or failed to do about it
3. Electricity OmbudsmanThe Ombudsman appointed by TERCThe CGRF order, or proof the forum missed its deadline

Two deadlines worth knowing. Under the central Electricity Rights of Consumers Rules a grievance should normally be decided within 30 days and in any case within 45. And if the paper you received is an assessment under Section 126 for unauthorised use rather than an ordinary bill, it does not go to the CGRF at all — it is appealed to the Appellate Authority under Section 127, usually within 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

How is the electricity bill calculated in Tripura?
Tripura bills combine slab-wise energy charges, a fixed/demand charge per kW (or kVA), a fuel & power purchase adjustment (FPPA) and electricity duty. Use our free calculator to get an itemised provisional bill for your DISCOM.
Which electricity distribution company serves my area in Tripura?
Tripura is served by 1 DISCOM: TSECL (Entire Tripura). Open your DISCOM above for its full tariff and an indicative bill.
What is the cheapest domestic electricity rate in Tripura?
The lowest domestic energy charge across Tripura DISCOMs starts at about ₹2.31 per unit (lowest slab), before fixed charges, FPPA and duty. Exact rates depend on your DISCOM and consumption slab.
Where do I complain about a wrong electricity bill in Tripura?
File first with your DISCOM — TSECL — or on the 24×7 helpline 1912, and keep the complaint number. If it is not resolved, escalate to your DISCOM's Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF) and then to the Electricity Ombudsman, both constituted under TERC regulations. A grievance should normally be decided within 30 days and in any case within 45.
What is the current electricity tariff year for Tripura?
The rates shown are for 2026-27 (latest available).
