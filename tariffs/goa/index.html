Who each rate applies to

LTDS-II — ordinary domestic (up to 85 kW / 100 kVA)
The standard household category: five telescopic slabs from ₹2.10 to ₹6.60, on ₹25 per kW (or part thereof) per month.
LTDS-I — lifeline (load up to 250 W and up to 50 units)
₹1.50/unit on a flat ₹20 per connection per month. Qualifying needs BOTH a connected load at or below 250 watts and consumption at or below 50 units.
LTDS-III — let-out portions and homestays
A slightly higher domestic schedule for the let-out part of a house: ₹3.15 to ₹6.70 across the same five slabs, on ₹30/kW/month. An owner-occupied home stays on LTDS-II; a homestay or let-out portion needs its own connection billed here.

ℹ️ Goa's domestic tariff is among the cheapest in the country at low consumption — ₹2.10 for the first 100 units — but climbs steeply, tripling to ₹6.60 above 400. Because JERC set the whole 4th control period in one order, the rates step up each year to FY 2029-30 regardless of any fresh order, so a Goa bill rises annually by design. Lifeline (LTDS-I) needs both a load at or below 250 W and consumption at or below 50 units. JERC's schedule is the tariff alone. Domestic bills here include Goa's 20 paise/unit electricity duty; other State levies are charged on the bill in addition. Goa's fixed charge is levied per kW (or kVA) 'or part thereof', so a fractional sanctioned load is rounded up to the next whole unit before the charge is worked out — the calculator does not round, so check your sanctioned load if the fixed charge looks a rupee or two light.

Who each rate applies to

NDS-I — non-domestic service
Three telescopic bands on kVAh — ₹4.25 up to 100 units, ₹6.10 to 200, ₹6.80 beyond — against a ₹60 per kVA per month fixed charge.
NDS-II — non-domestic service
The same ₹60/kVA fixed charge and the same first two bands as NDS-I, but ₹6.90 above 200 units instead of ₹6.80.
NDS-III — non-domestic service (highest rate)
A single ₹11.65/kVAh on all units against ₹75/kVA/month — by some distance the steepest rate in Goa's LT schedule.
NDS-IV — non-domestic service (concessional)
A flat ₹4.75/kVAh on all units against ₹60/kVA/month.

ℹ️ Non-domestic supply is billed on kVAh against a per-kVA fixed charge, so a poor power factor raises the units you are charged for. JERC splits non-domestic into several schedules whose rates differ sharply — from ₹4.75 flat on NDS-IV to ₹11.65 on NDS-III — so which one your connection sits under matters more than your consumption does. JERC's schedule is the tariff alone. Domestic bills here include Goa's 20 paise/unit electricity duty; other State levies are charged on the bill in addition. Goa's fixed charge is levied per kW (or kVA) 'or part thereof', so a fractional sanctioned load is rounded up to the next whole unit before the charge is worked out — the calculator does not round, so check your sanctioned load if the fixed charge looks a rupee or two light.

Wrong bill in Goa? Where to complain

Complain in this order and keep the complaint number at every step. Skipping a step is the commonest reason a genuine billing complaint is returned unheard.

StepWhere it goesWhat you need
1. DISCOM complaint deskGoa Electricity Dept., or the 24×7 helpline 1912Consumer number, bill number, the one disputed line, and a meter photo
2. Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF)Your DISCOM's forum, constituted under JERC-UTS regulationsThe first complaint number, and what the DISCOM did or failed to do about it
3. Electricity OmbudsmanOffice of the Electricity Ombudsman, appointed by JERC-UTSThe CGRF order, or proof the forum missed its deadline

Two deadlines worth knowing. Under the central Electricity Rights of Consumers Rules a grievance should normally be decided within 30 days and in any case within 45. And if the paper you received is an assessment under Section 126 for unauthorised use rather than an ordinary bill, it does not go to the CGRF at all — it is appealed to the Appellate Authority under Section 127, usually within 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

How is the electricity bill calculated in Goa?
Goa bills combine slab-wise energy charges, a fixed/demand charge per kW (or kVA), a fuel & power purchase adjustment (FPPA) and electricity duty. Use our free calculator to get an itemised provisional bill for your DISCOM.
Which electricity distribution company serves my area in Goa?
Goa is served by 1 DISCOM: Goa Electricity Dept. (Entire Goa). Open your DISCOM above for its full tariff and an indicative bill.
What is the cheapest domestic electricity rate in Goa?
The lowest domestic energy charge across Goa DISCOMs starts at about ₹1.5 per unit (lowest slab), before fixed charges, FPPA and duty. Exact rates depend on your DISCOM and consumption slab.
Where do I complain about a wrong electricity bill in Goa?
File first with your DISCOM — Goa Electricity Dept. — or on the 24×7 helpline 1912, and keep the complaint number. If it is not resolved, escalate to your DISCOM's Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF) and then to the Electricity Ombudsman, both constituted under JERC-UTS regulations. A grievance should normally be decided within 30 days and in any case within 45.
What is the current electricity tariff year for Goa?
The rates shown are for 2026-27 (latest available).
