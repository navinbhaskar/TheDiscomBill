ℹ️ Punjab picks the domestic band by sanctioned load, not by consumption, and the slabs inside a band are telescopic — the first 300 units at the band's own rate, everything above at ₹7.05. Statutory levies (electricity duty, cow cess, infrastructure development fee) are notified by the State Government rather than by PSERC and are NOT included in this calculation — your actual bill will carry them.

Frequently asked questions

How is the electricity bill calculated in Punjab?
Punjab bills combine slab-wise energy charges, a fixed/demand charge per kW (or kVA), a fuel & power purchase adjustment (FPPA) and electricity duty. Use our free calculator to get an itemised provisional bill for your DISCOM.
Which electricity distribution company serves my area in Punjab?
Punjab is served by 1 DISCOM: PSPCL (Entire Punjab — excl. Chandigarh). Open your DISCOM above for its full tariff and an indicative bill.
What is the cheapest domestic electricity rate in Punjab?
The lowest domestic energy charge across Punjab DISCOMs starts at about ₹3.85 per unit (lowest slab), before fixed charges, FPPA and duty. Exact rates depend on your DISCOM and consumption slab.
What is the current electricity tariff year for Punjab?
The rates shown are for 2026-27 (latest available).
