Frequently asked questions

How is the electricity bill calculated in Meghalaya?
Meghalaya bills combine slab-wise energy charges, a fixed/demand charge per kW (or kVA), a fuel & power purchase adjustment (FPPA) and electricity duty. Use our free calculator to get an itemised provisional bill for your DISCOM.
Which electricity distribution company serves my area in Meghalaya?
Meghalaya is served by 1 DISCOM: MePDCL (Entire Meghalaya). Open your DISCOM above for its full tariff and an indicative bill.
What is the cheapest domestic electricity rate in Meghalaya?
The lowest domestic energy charge across Meghalaya DISCOMs starts at about ₹4.57 per unit (lowest slab), before fixed charges, FPPA and duty. Exact rates depend on your DISCOM and consumption slab.
What is the current electricity tariff year for Meghalaya?
The rates shown are for 2026-27 (latest available).
