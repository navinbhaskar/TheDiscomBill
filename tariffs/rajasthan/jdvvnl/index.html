ℹ️ Fixed charges are banded on average monthly consumption, not sanctioned load. A Regulatory Surcharge of ₹1.00/unit applies (₹0.70/unit for households consuming up to 100 units a month) — it is a combined ceiling that already includes the Fuel and Power Purchase Adjustment Surcharge, so FPPAS is not charged on top of it. BPL and Astha card holders and silicosis patients pay 475 paise on the first 50 units with a ₹150 fixed charge.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my JdVVNL electricity bill?
Open the JdVVNL bill calculator, enter your units consumed and sanctioned load, and it applies the 2025-26 JdVVNL slab rates, fixed charge and other charges to produce an itemised provisional bill.
What is the cheapest domestic electricity rate on JdVVNL?
JdVVNL's domestic energy charge starts at ₹4.75 per unit and rises to ₹7.5 per unit in the highest slab (DS/LT-1 (Domestic Service)), plus a monthly fixed charge (latest available estimate). The full slab table is above.
How do I pay my JdVVNL electricity bill online?
Pay on the official JdVVNL portal at energy.rajasthan.gov.in/jdvvnl. Use this page first to check what your bill should be, then pay on the authentic source.
