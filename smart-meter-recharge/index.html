How a prepaid recharge works

The mechanics are much the same at every DISCOM — what differs is the portal and the low-balance rules. Pick yours below for the same steps written against its own portal.

  1. Find your consumer or meter numberIt is on the meter display, or at the top of your last bill.
  2. Use an official channelThe DISCOM’s own portal or app, or a BBPS-enabled UPI app.
  3. Pay, then check the balanceIt usually appears on the meter within minutes.
  4. Keep a bufferRecharge before it reaches zero — disconnection rules vary by DISCOM.

Frequently asked questions

How does a smart meter recharge work?
Enter your consumer/meter number on your DISCOM's official portal/app or a BBPS-enabled UPI app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm), pick an amount and pay. The balance usually updates within minutes. Pick your DISCOM above — each page gives the exact channel.
Is power cut immediately when a prepaid smart meter balance runs out?
Supply can be disconnected automatically on a negative balance, but most DISCOMs do not disconnect at night, on Sundays or holidays. Once you recharge, supply is normally restored automatically.
Does a smart meter bill more than a normal meter?
No — the rates stay exactly as per the tariff order. The real reasons readings jump (an old meter under-reading, arrears adjustment and more) are covered in our guide.
