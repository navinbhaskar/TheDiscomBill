Solar Subsidy Checker (PM Surya Ghar)

The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana pays up to ₹78,000 towards a home rooftop-solar system — but the portal never tells you what size you need or what it saves. Enter your state and monthly usage and we'll size the system, show the exact central subsidy, your net cost after it, and the yearly savings and payback at your DISCOM's real rate.

How the PM Surya Ghar subsidy is calculated

The central subsidy is fixed by system size, not by your income or bill: ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW — so a 1 kW system gets ₹30,000, 2 kW gets ₹60,000, and anything 3 kW or larger is capped at ₹78,000. Many states add their own top-up on top of this central amount. You register on the national portal, pick an empanelled vendor, and the subsidy is credited to your bank account after the system is installed and the net meter is commissioned.

What size do you actually need?

As a rule of thumb, 1 kW covers about 120 units a month and needs roughly 100 sq ft of shadow-free roof, generating around 4 units a day. Sizing the system to your own consumption is what makes solar pay — oversizing past what you use (and past the 3 kW subsidy cap) just lengthens the payback. For the full net-metering and banking maths on your bill, use the rooftop solar savings calculator; to see your current bill first, open the bill calculator.

The central PM Surya Ghar subsidy slab is fixed nationally and used exactly; system cost (~₹55,000/kW) and generation (~4 units/kW/day) are all-India benchmarks and your actual quote and site will differ. State top-up subsidies vary and change often. This is a planning estimate — confirm current amounts, eligibility and empanelled vendors on the official PM Surya Ghar portal before committing.
