How does it work?

  1. You enter your average monthly bill — we work back to your consumption in units.
  2. We recommend a system size in kW that offsets that consumption, limited by your roof at 100 sq ft per kW.
  3. We apply the 2026 PM Surya Ghar scale: ₹30K for 1 kW, ₹60K for 2 kW, ₹78K for 3 kW and above.
  4. Yearly generation assumed at 4 kWh per kW per day — a realistic India average across seasons.
  5. Savings = yearly generation × your ₹/unit. Payback = net cost ÷ yearly savings.

The year-by-year table above is the careful version of the same sum: it adds ~5% a year of tariff inflation and ~0.7% a year of panel degradation, which these flat figures ignore.
