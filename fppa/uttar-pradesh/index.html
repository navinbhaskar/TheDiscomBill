Why did it change?

UPPCL publishes the FPPAS rate monthly as a percentage of fixed plus energy charges. The rate can be positive when fuel and power-purchase costs are recovered, or negative when the adjustment is passed back as a credit.

Official source

  • UPPCL monthly FPPAS notice (UPERC MYT Reg. 2025)
